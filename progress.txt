DECISIONS
---------
- Accent Color: Selected warm editorial amber (oklch(0.78 0.16 68) dark / oklch(0.58 0.18 55) light) as the single accent color, keeping all other tokens neutral zinc/slate grays for a clean, minimal editorial aesthetic.
- Dark Default: Configured next-themes ThemeProvider with defaultTheme="dark", enableSystem=true, and mapped default tokens to :root and .dark to eliminate any white flash during SSR/hydration.
- Environment Validation: Implemented lib/env.ts using Zod with a lazy validation Proxy and safe fallback check (SKIP_ENV_VALIDATION / test) to ensure rock-solid type safety without failing build-time page generation if secrets are unpopulated.
- Route Group Structure: Placed the home page in app/(public)/page.tsx matching PRD section 12 target structure.
- Prettier & ESLint Integration: Integrated prettier-plugin-tailwindcss and eslint-config-prettier for consistent class ordering and zero formatting conflicts.
